About Us

Family-owned, locally trusted, built on hard work

Steve's Towing

We treat every customer like family

At Steve's Towing, we believe towing is about more than moving a vehicle. It's about helping people when they need it most.

For decades, our family-owned company has proudly served Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Charlotte County, and surrounding communities with dependable towing and roadside assistance. We understand that being stranded, involved in an accident, or dealing with a vehicle emergency can be stressful. That's why our goal is simple: treat every customer with honesty, respect, compassion, and care.

We take pride in treating every vehicle as if it were our own and in providing professional service from the moment you call until the job is complete.

At Steve's Towing, our customers aren't just customers. They're our neighbors and part of our community. We are grateful for the trust that local families, businesses, repair facilities, dealerships, and law enforcement have placed in us throughout the years.

When you need a tow, you need someone you can trust.

Steve's Towing; here when you need us, because you never know when you'll need us.
