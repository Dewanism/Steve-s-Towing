About Us

Family-owned, locally trusted, built on hard work

Steve's Towing has been serving drivers and property owners across Southwest Florida for over 20 years. What started as a family-owned operation has grown into a name the community relies on for reliable, professional towing and roadside help, without losing the personal touch that got us here.

Family-owned and operated

We're not a call center dispatching to a stranger. Steve's Towing is a family business, and every job gets handled with the same care we'd want for our own vehicles. That approach has kept customers calling us back, and referring their neighbors, for more than two decades.

Serving Southwest Florida

We serve Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Englewood, North Port, Venice, Rotonda, Arcadia, Fort Myers, and nearby communities, with fast response when drivers need it most.
