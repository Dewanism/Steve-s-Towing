What Areas of Venice Do You Serve?

Steve's Towing covers all of Venice, including historic Downtown and West Venice Avenue on Venice Island, the Jacaranda Boulevard and US-41 Bypass corridor, South Venice, and the Wellen Park master-planned community. We also handle calls at the Circus Bridge and Hatchett Creek Bridge crossings onto the island, plus I-75 access points via Venice Avenue and Jacaranda Boulevard.

Why Choose Steve's Towing in Venice?

Venice is split by the Intracoastal Waterway, which means getting on and off Venice Island depends on timing drawbridges right — something out-of-town tow companies don't always account for. Our operators work this route regularly, so a breakdown near the downtown shops on West Venice Avenue gets a truck that already knows the fastest way across. We use flatbed and wheel-lift equipment depending on the job, whether that's a careful transport downtown or a quick clear off US-41.

Do You Offer Emergency Towing at Night in Venice?

Yes. Steve's Towing runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week throughout Venice, from a late-night dead battery near Venice Beach to an early jump start before the Jacaranda Boulevard commute.
