Cash for Junk Cars

Turn your unwanted vehicle into cash

Got an old, damaged, or non-running vehicle taking up space? Steve's Towing buys junk cars in any condition and makes the process quick and hassle-free. Whether the engine has failed, repairs cost more than the vehicle is worth, or you just need fast cash, we'll make you a fair offer. Once we agree on a price, all you need is the vehicle title and we'll handle the rest.

We buy vehicles with

  • Blown or seized engines
  • High mileage
  • Accident damage
  • Failed transmission
  • Non-running vehicles
  • Flood or fire damage
  • Scrap vehicles
  • Vehicles that cost too much to repair

Why sell your car?

  • Free up driveway or garage space

  • Environmentally friendly

How it works

  1. 1

    Request a quote

    Call or message us with your vehicle's year, make, model, condition, and location.

  2. 2

    Receive an offer

    We'll provide a competitive cash offer based on your vehicle's condition and current scrap value.

  3. 3

    Bring your title

    To complete the sale, you'll need the vehicle title.

  4. 4

    Get paid

    We'll purchase your vehicle and transport it to a licensed junk yard, where it is recycled and sold by weight.

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy vehicles?

Yes, we purchase running and non-running vehicles in any condition.

Do I need a vehicle title?

Yes, you'll need the vehicle title to complete the sale.

What happens to my vehicle?

After purchase, your vehicle will be transported to a licensed junk yard, where it is dismantled, recycled, or sold for scrap.
