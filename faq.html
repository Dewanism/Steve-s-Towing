General questions

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes. We offer a military and veterans discount as a thank-you for your service. Just let us know when you call, and we'll go over the details.

How much does a tow cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle, the distance, and the type of service you need. Call us at 941-769-4187 and we'll give you a quote.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

Do you offer business accounts?

Yes. Businesses can open a credit account and be billed monthly. Download the account application (PDF), fill it out, and fax it to (941) 624-4167. See Business Accounts for details.

What are your hours?

We're open every day, Monday through Sunday, from 8AM to 12AM (midnight).

What areas do you serve?

We serve Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Englewood, North Port, Venice, Rotonda West, Arcadia, Fort Myers, and nearby communities across Southwest Florida. If you don't see your area, call us to confirm whether we can reach you.

How do I request service?

Call us at 941-769-4187 with your location, your vehicle's make and model, and a brief description of the situation.

Are you a local, family-owned company?

Yes. Steve's Towing is family-owned and operated, and has proudly served Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Charlotte County, and surrounding communities for decades.

Towing

Learn more about towing

What vehicles do you tow?

We tow a wide range of vehicles, including cars, trucks, SUVs, motorcycles, and specialty vehicles like classics, antiques, and performance cars.

Can you tow my vehicle to a specific location?

Yes, we can tow your vehicle to a specific location of your choice, whether it's a repair shop, your home, or another destination.

Do you provide emergency towing services?

Yes, we offer emergency towing services for all types of vehicles during our business hours.

What if my vehicle won't move?

If your vehicle won't move, our team is trained to safely load and tow it using specialized equipment, ensuring it reaches its destination without further damage.

Lockouts

Learn more about lockouts

What lockout situations can you help with?

We can help with keys locked inside your vehicle or trunk, lost or misplaced keys, and accidental lockouts.

What vehicles can you unlock?

We help with cars, SUVs, pickup trucks, and light-duty vehicles.

Will unlocking my car damage it?

We use professional lockout equipment and damage-free entry techniques designed to minimize the risk of damage to your vehicle.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your location, your vehicle's make and model, and a brief description of the situation. A trained technician will arrive with the proper tools to unlock your vehicle.

Roadside assistance

Learn more about roadside assistance

What types of roadside assistance do you provide?

We offer jump starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery, vehicle lockout assistance, and other minor roadside services.

What if my vehicle can't be repaired on-site?

If the problem can't be resolved safely at your location, we can tow your vehicle to a repair shop, dealership, or another destination of your choice.

Can you help with all vehicle types?

We assist most cars, SUVs, pickup trucks, and light-duty vehicles.

Shed & toolbox transport

Learn more about shed & toolbox transport

What size sheds can you move?

We transport many types of storage sheds. Contact us with the dimensions, and we'll let you know if we can accommodate your move.

Can you transport large jobsite toolboxes?

Yes. We regularly move heavy toolboxes and other equipment for homeowners, contractors, and businesses.

Do I need to empty the shed or toolbox first?

In most cases, yes. Removing loose items helps reduce weight and prevents damage during transport.

Do you offer residential and commercial transport?

Absolutely. We serve homeowners, contractors, and businesses throughout the area.

Private property towing

Learn more about private property towing

Who can request a private property tow?

Property owners, business owners, property managers, and individuals authorized to manage a private parking area.

Do I need signs posted on my property?

Towing requirements vary depending on local and state regulations. We can help you understand the requirements for your property.

Can you remove abandoned vehicles?

Yes. We can assist with abandoned vehicles, provided the necessary legal requirements are met.

Do you offer ongoing parking enforcement?

Yes. We work with businesses, apartment complexes, and property managers to help manage unauthorized parking.

Cash for junk cars

Learn more about cash for junk cars

Do you buy vehicles?

Yes, we purchase running and non-running vehicles in any condition.

Do I need a vehicle title?

Yes, you'll need the vehicle title to complete the sale.

What happens to my vehicle?

After purchase, your vehicle will be transported to a licensed junk yard, where it is dismantled, recycled, or sold for scrap.
