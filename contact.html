Directions to our impound lot

Coming to pick up your vehicle? Here’s the easiest route from I-75.

  1. 1

    Take I-75 to Exit 170

    Exit onto Kings Highway and head toward Harbor View Road.

  2. 2

    Turn left onto Harbor View Road

    Continue about ½ to ¾ of a mile.

  3. 3

    Turn left onto Whidden Boulevard

    Look for the Circle K on the corner.

  4. 4

    Turn right onto Janice Avenue

    At the end of Whidden Boulevard. Our impound and storage lot is on the right, just before the curve.
